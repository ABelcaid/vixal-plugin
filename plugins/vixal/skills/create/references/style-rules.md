# Style Rules

Use this reference when producing Vixal-ready story, page, panel, character, or image-generation prompts.

## Manga

Use for Japanese manga style.

Visual rules:
- Strict black-and-white grayscale.
- Black ink lineart with screentone shading.
- No color accents, neon, or colored lighting.
- Hand-drawn finish with G-pen or maru-pen feel, organic line wobble, and strong lineweight variation.
- Use crosshatching in deep shadows and textured black fills.
- Paper should be pure white, not yellowed, sepia, beige, or parchment.

Layout rules:
- 6 to 8 panels per page by default.
- Dynamic, overlapping, irregular layouts.
- Expressive gutters and cinematic flow.
- Right-to-left reading direction.

SFX rules:
- Use manga-style graphic SFX. If writing in English, describe it as katakana-style lettering rather than inventing untranslated Japanese text.

## Manhwa

Use for Korean manhwa style.

Visual rules:
- Full color with soft digital painting.
- Clean thin lineart, smooth cel shading or soft gradients.
- Glossy hair, eyes, and highlights.
- Harmonized color palette with cinematic lighting, colored rim light, ambient glow, and haze when useful.
- Backgrounds may be semi-realistic or photorealistic with depth-of-field blur.

Layout rules:
- Vertical scroll canvas.
- 1 to 3 panels per screen view.
- Top-to-bottom flow with generous white-space gaps.
- Left-to-right reading inside panels.

SFX rules:
- Integrate SFX as polished graphic design elements.

## Western Comics

Use for American or European comics.

Visual rules:
- Full color.
- Use bold saturated palettes for American superhero tone, or muted naturalistic palettes for European bande dessinee tone.
- Heavy black ink outlines, confident lineweight, hatching for shadow, and strong chiaroscuro.
- American style can use Ben-Day dots or flat digital colors.
- European style can use watercolor, gouache, or clean ligne claire influence.
- Backgrounds should have strong architectural or environmental clarity.

Layout rules:
- 4 to 7 panels per page by default.
- Rectangular grid-based layouts such as 3x2 or 2x3, with occasional inset panels.
- Clear black gutters.
- Left-to-right, top-to-bottom reading direction.
- Use splash pages or double-page-spread logic for key moments only.

SFX rules:
- Use hand-lettered SFX that interact with action and panel composition.

## Webtoon

Use for digital vertical-scroll storytelling.

Visual rules:
- Full color optimized for mobile reading.
- Use clear silhouettes, high contrast, and readable facial acting.
- Style can lean manhwa-soft-painting or flat graphic illustration; ask for sub-style if it changes the output.
- Use simplified backgrounds for dialogue scenes and detailed backgrounds for establishing or dramatic moments.
- Motion blur, glow, particle effects, and other digital effects are allowed.

Layout rules:
- Strict top-to-bottom vertical scroll.
- Usually one major panel per screen for dramatic beats.
- Use white space, color blocks, or scenic transitions rather than boxed page gutters.
- Left-to-right reading inside panels.

SFX rules:
- Integrate SFX into the scroll as graphic design, not as detached labels.

## Dialogue And Captions

For all traditions:
- Put dialogue only in speech balloons.
- Use `Speaker:` labels for clarity.
- Separate dialogue, captions, and SFX.
- Keep captions purposeful: time, location, internal thought, or transition.
- Do not use dialogue to explain what the image already shows clearly.
